# Privacy policy – CarKo (English)

Last change: 24 Oct 2026

CarKo is an Android app for the costs, fuel and service of your car. This is the same text as in the app ("Settings → Privacy policy").

## What data there is and where it stays

- **No accounts.** Your cars, costs, fill-ups and service schedule are kept only in the phone's memory.
- If backup to your Google account is on, Android copies this data too, as it does for other apps.
- **Receipts.** They are captured with the Google Play services scanner and read on the phone itself with ML Kit. The photo is not uploaded anywhere.
- **Backup, Excel and CSV.** The files are saved only where you save or send them. With “Automatic backup” on, every week to the file you picked. A file you import (Fuelio, Drivvo, a spreadsheet) is read on the phone only.
- **The year card.** It is made on the phone and goes only where you share it.
- **Vignette check.** Only when you tap “Check with BGTOLL” in “Service”, the registration plate goes to check.bgtoll.bg of the Road Infrastructure Agency; only the vignette's dates are saved in the schedule. Nothing is checked by itself.
- **Reminders.** They are worked out on the phone. No data is sent.

## Fuel prices (optional)

While price sharing is on (the default; turned off with the switch in the fill-up form or in "Settings → Share fuel prices"): when you save a fill-up with a station picked from the list, and when you report prices, the station, the town, the fuel, the price, whether it came from a receipt and the time go to CarKo's server (Google Firebase, Frankfurt, EU) with an anonymous ID that Firebase creates for the app. No name, car, mileage or location. Other drivers see the prices. The week's points stay on the phone. The station is also recognised from the receipt: the till's number and the station's name and address from the receipt are remembered on the phone and, if you share, go to the server together with the station, so other drivers' phones recognise it too.

## Stations

The list comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL licence). If you add or fix a station, its name, brand, town and address go to CarKo's server with the same anonymous ID. With "Also send to OpenStreetMap" the fix becomes an anonymous OpenStreetMap note at the position on their map. "Search Google" sends what you typed to Google Places (https://policies.google.com/privacy); CarKo keeps only Google's place ID for the directions button.

## Location

Only if you allow it with the "Nearby" button, and only on the phone: it sorts the stations by distance and picks the town. It is not stored and not sent anywhere.

## Ads

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. For the ads Google may use the device's advertising ID, the IP address and device data, including for personalized ads if you consented.

In the European Union consent is asked on first launch (a Google form) and can be changed at any time in "Settings → Ad consent".

How Google uses the data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

So that the same ads pay more, AppLovin and Meta Audience Network also bid in AdMob's auction. They get the same advertising data and are listed in the consent form. Their rules: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/ and https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

## Statistics (Google Analytics)

Google Analytics for Firebase counts how the app is used (screens, sessions, ad revenue) and three CarKo Pro events (the card shown, the sheet opened, a purchase). It starts only after the consent form and only if you allow data storage; it uses the advertising ID and an internal app ID. Your cars, records, places and anything you type are never sent. Firebase Remote Config may change how often ads show, always within set limits.

## "No ads"

Ads can be turned off for 6 months for 10 friends who install CarKo from your link, or for a month for 10 minutes of watched ads. The link carries a random code. When the friend opens CarKo for the first time, the app takes the code from Google Play and sends the server the code and a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the app's internal ID on their phone, so each phone counts once. No names are sent. Shared fuel prices earn days without ads (3 points in a week = 7 days); the counter stays on the phone.

## CarKo Pro ("No ads, forever")

CarKo Pro removes the ads for good and allows more than 2 cars and unlimited exports. It is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment and its data under its own rules (https://policies.google.com/privacy); CarKo never sees your card. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase was made, on every start, and keeps the answer on the phone. That is why the purchase also works on a new phone with the same Google account.

## Deletion

The anonymous data on the server cannot be linked to you. For questions and deletion, write to the address below.
